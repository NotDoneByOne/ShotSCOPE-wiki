Weapons, ammunition, equipment, and accessories for all shooting disciplines, from rifle shooting to practical and clay target shooting.

## Weapons

- [Rifles](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Rifles): air and smallbore rifles, with design, setup, zeroing, and maintenance.
- [Pistols](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Pistols): sport pistols, with grip and trigger mechanisms.

## Equipment

- [Optics and sights](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Optics): diopter and optical sights, with setup, alignment, and maintenance.
- [Clothing and protection](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Clothing): jackets, trousers, gloves, ear protection, glasses, and ISSF standards.
- [Shooting mats and slings](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Accessories): slings, mats, rests, and cushions.
- [Ammunition](/en/Weapons%20and%20equipment/Ammunition): bullets, cartridges, powder, and primers, with selection, storage, and testing.

Competition rules set the requirements for weapons and equipment: [Rules and regulations](/en/Rules%20and%20regulations).

## Weapon records in ShotSCOPE

In **My weapon** on the home screen, you can record the model and serial number, sight settings for different conditions, zeroing parameters, maintenance history, and notes. Results link to a specific weapon.
