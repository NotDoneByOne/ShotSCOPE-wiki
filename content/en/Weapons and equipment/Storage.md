Proper storage protects a weapon from corrosion and deformation, and the people around it from accidents. The key conditions are dryness, stable temperature, and restricted access.

> [!warning] Law
> Requirements for storing and transporting weapons and ammunition are set by the laws of your country. In Russia, they are set by Federal Law No. 150-FZ of December 13, 1996, "On Weapons" ([official text](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_12793/) (in Russian), [legal information portal](http://pravo.gov.ru) (in Russian)), and by Rosgvardia requirements.

## Dryness and temperature

Moisture is the main enemy of metal. Keep the weapon in a dry room without sharp temperature swings, which cause condensation on metal. Do not leave it for long in a car trunk, on a balcony, or in a damp basement.

After shooting in the cold or the rain, let the weapon warm up to room temperature inside its case, then wipe it dry. The closed case helps here: condensation settles on it instead of on the metal.

## Cases and placement

A case protects the weapon from dust, impacts, and scratches to the sights, optics, and barrel. Choose a case that fits the weapon, and never put damp items in it.

- Do not store a weapon in a case made of moisture-retaining material unless you are sure the weapon is dry.
- Do not stand a weapon on its optics, muzzle, or trigger guard.
- Do not put heavy objects on top of the weapon or optics.
- Before long-term storage, clean and lubricate the weapon following the [[en/Weapons and equipment/Barrel cleaning|instructions]].

## Ammunition

Store ammunition separately from the weapon, in factory packaging or dedicated boxes, in a dry, cool place. Keep it away from heat and moisture, corrosive substances, and open flame.

## Access for others

Weapons and ammunition must be out of reach of children and anyone not authorized to use them. Use a lockable place and keep the keys separate. No convenience is worth that risk.

## Equipment and optics

Store the jacket and trousers laid out flat in a dry place, and dry the glove and shoes after use. Keep optics in a case, away from direct light and moisture.
