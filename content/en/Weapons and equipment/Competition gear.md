Packing for a match is easier with a list. Go through it the day before, not on competition day, so you have time to buy or fix anything missing.

## What you need

- The competition regulations and schedule.
- The organizer's requirements for weapons and equipment.

## Checklist

### Weapon

- [ ] Weapon in its case, cleaned and maintained per the instructions.
- [ ] Sights set to your zeroing.
- [ ] Spare parts and tools you may need.

### Ammunition

- [ ] Ammunition with a reserve for sighting and scored series.
- [ ] Ammunition from the same lot as in training.

### Equipment

- [ ] Jacket, trousers, glove, and shoes that comply with the rules.
- [ ] Sling, rests, and other accessories for your discipline.
- [ ] Hearing and eye protection.
- [ ] Spare clothing for the weather and in case you get wet.

### Documents

- [ ] The documents the organizer requires.
- [ ] Medical clearance: you can check your status in **Medical check-up** in the app.

### Small items

- [ ] Water and a light snack.
- [ ] A notebook or the ShotSCOPE app to record series.
- [ ] A charged phone.

> [!warning] Transport
> The law sets the rules for transporting weapons and ammunition. Check them in advance, and see [[en/Weapons and equipment/Storage|Storage]] for more.
