Shooting clothing helps you hold a position steadily and reduces fatigue, but it does not replace technique. Competition rules limit your choices, so check the ISSF requirements before you buy.

> [!warning] Rules
> Equipment requirements, such as thickness, stiffness, and construction, are described in the ISSF technical rules. Check the current edition on the [ISSF website](https://www.issf-sports.org) and ask your coach or the organizer.

## What equipment is for

The jacket, trousers, and shoes stabilize your body position and spread the load. Well-chosen equipment helps you relax your muscles and hold a position longer. It works only when your position and training are sound.

## Main items

### Jacket

A shooting jacket supports the torso and arm in prone, kneeling, and standing. It must comply with the rules on materials and construction. Choose it to fit your build so it does not restrict breathing.

### Trousers

Trousers help in kneeling and standing. Choose them so they do not hinder movement or add stiffness beyond what the rules allow.

### Glove

The glove protects the hand from sling pressure and helps you hold the weapon comfortably. Its size and construction are also checked against the rules.

### Shoes

Shoes for standing give a stable base. The sole should be flat and the last stable. For pistol, choose comfortable, non-slip shoes.

## How to choose

The choice depends on discipline, height, build, and budget. Try equipment on in position, not while standing at a mirror. Beginners do not need expensive sets right away: start with the basics and discuss your choice with your coach.

- Try each item in your working position and check for pressure points.
- Make sure you can still breathe freely.
- Check that the item complies with the rules for your competitions.
- Ask the organizer whether equipment is checked before the start.

## Care

Dry your equipment after training, do not wash it unless the manufacturer says so, and store it laid out flat. For more on storage, see [[en/Weapons and equipment/Storage|Storage]].
