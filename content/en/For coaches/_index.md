A section for those who help others become better: materials that make a coach's work more effective and more mindful.

## What's inside

- **Training methodology** — how to build a training program for different levels and goals.
- **Working with a team** — the psychology of interaction, motivation, and the atmosphere in the group.
- **Individual approach** — how to notice each athlete's traits and adapt training to their style.
- **Professional growth** — new research in sports science and exchanging experience with colleagues.

## Share your methods

The best methods are born in dialogue. If you have methods you can share with colleagues, read [[en/Community/How to become an author|How to become an author]].
