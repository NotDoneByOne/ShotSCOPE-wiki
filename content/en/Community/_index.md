The ShotSCOPE community is athletes, coaches, judges and organizers, developers, and knowledge base authors. Here you can ask a question, suggest an idea, and write an article.

## How to take part

- [How to become an author](/en/Community/How%20to%20become%20an%20author) — write an article: your experience, an exercise breakdown, competition preparation.
- Question or idea — email [notdonebyone@internet.ru](mailto:notdonebyone@internet.ru); we read every message.
- Community event announcements — in the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/shotscope_chat).
- Tell your friends, your coach, and your colleagues at the range about ShotSCOPE SPM.

## What users ask for

Users most often ask for saving targets with notes, a diary for tasks and mistakes, result charts for EVSK exercises (date, result, comment), and data recovery when changing devices. Some of this is already in development, and users' ideas shape the updates.
