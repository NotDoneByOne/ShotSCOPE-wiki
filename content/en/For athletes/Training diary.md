The ShotSCOPE diary puts training and competitions into one history: from an event down to a single shot on a target. From your entries the app calculates statistics, grouping, and category compliance.

## Events and sessions

An *event* is a training day or a competition. An event has a name, a date, a discipline, and a weapon. You cannot create an event for a future date.

*Sessions* live inside an event: each one describes one part of the day. Sessions come in three groups.

- **Training**: **Dry fire**, **Dry fire with marking**, **Conditioning**.
- **Competitive**: **Control**, **Competition**, **Sighting**.
- **Theoretical**: **Theory**.

The sessions of an event are listed on its card and counted together. An event must have at least one session.

## Series, targets, and shots

A *series* is a group of shots you enter on one *target*. In a session with targets, you open a target card and mark every *shot*: the app records its value, its position relative to the center, and the time since the previous shot.

Before you save a session, the **Target review** opens. For each target it shows the number of shots, the average score, and accuracy. The app deletes targets without marks.

## Sighting and scored shots

In a competitive session, targets come in two kinds. The sighting target is labeled **Trial**, and the scored ones are **Scored no. 1**, **Scored no. 2**, and so on. Sighting confirms that the weapon hits where it should and does not count toward the result. The separate **Sighting** session lets you practice this before the start.

> [!note] Label on screen
> The knowledge base calls these sighting and scored shots; the app shows **Trial** and **Scored**.

## Statistics and grouping

After entering data, open **Session statistics**. It contains:

- **Session result**: total shots, sum, average score, and average grouping;
- **Distribution** of hits;
- **By target**: a chart comparing the targets;
- **Category compliance**: your result against the standard and the gap to the next one.

*Grouping* shows how tightly your shots landed. *MPI* (mean point of impact) is the center of the shot group. On a target you can see the distance from a shot to the target center, to the MPI, and to the MPI by grouping.

The general **Statistics** screen adds up all entries: shots, series, events, weapons, competitions, and conditioning.

## Weapons and sight adjustments

Weapons are kept under **My weapons**: name, serial number, type, and linked exercises. You cannot create an event without a weapon and a linked discipline.

Each weapon has a log of **Sight adjustments**: X and Y offsets, date, and place. The adjustment you mark as the **Zeroing point** becomes zero, and the offsets of the others are recalculated. You cannot delete the only adjustment.

## Reports

A PDF report for an event or a period is built from your diary entries. Tap **Build a report**: the finished file stays in the app, and you can send it to your coach by email.
