A section for those who work on their results every day, from a first shot to a master's title.

## What's inside

- **Training process** — how to structure training so that progress is steady rather than random.
- **Psychology** — how to cope with nerves at competitions, get ready for the shot, and avoid burnout.
- **Mistake analysis** — typical problems and ways to fix them.
- **Equipment** — how to choose gear to suit you, not just "like everyone else's".
