The ShotSCOPE Knowledge Base is an open collection of materials for shooters, coaches, and judges: from app guides to coaching methods, competition rules, and anti-doping. We are the ShotSCOPE team: students, coaches, and athletes who believe that information should be open and progress should be measurable.

## Where to start

- **Athlete.** Materials for training: [[en/For athletes/_index|For athletes]], [[en/For athletes/Training diary|training diary]].
- **Coach.** Methods and work with a team: [[en/For coaches/_index|For coaches]].
- **Judge or organizer.** Rules and regulations: [[en/Rules and regulations/_index|Rules and regulations]].
- **App user.** Guides and releases: [[en/ShotSCOPE/_index|ShotSCOPE]].
- **Developer, author.** How the project works and how to write an article: [[en/Community/_index|Community]].

Other sections: [[en/Legal documents/_index|Legal documents]], [[en/Anti-doping/_index|Anti-doping]], [[en/Weapons and equipment/_index|Weapons and equipment]].

New to ShotSCOPE? Read [[en/ShotSCOPE/First steps|"First steps in ShotSCOPE"]] and follow the [[en/ShotSCOPE/Releases/_index|releases]].

## How to find what you need

Press `Ctrl+K` (`Cmd+K` on Mac): search covers all articles. Articles on a topic are grouped by [[en/tags/_index|tags]], and the folder structure is in the menu on the left.

## How to help the project

You can write an article, fix a mistake, or suggest a topic through an Issue or a Pull Request. Details are in the [[en/Community/How to become an author|guide for contributors]]; formatting rules are in the [style guide](https://github.com/NotDoneByOne/ShotSCOPE-wiki/blob/v4/STYLE_GUIDE.md) (in Russian) and [article templates](https://github.com/NotDoneByOne/ShotSCOPE-wiki/blob/v4/TEMPLATES.md) (in Russian).
