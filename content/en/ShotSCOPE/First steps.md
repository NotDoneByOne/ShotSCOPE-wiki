Install the app, create a profile, and record your first training session: it takes a few minutes from installation to a saved series.

## What you need

- A phone with RuStore.
- An email address: the sign-in code is sent there.
- The weapon you shoot with: add it before your first event.

## Installation and sign-in

1. Install [ShotSCOPE from RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.ndo.shotscope) (in Russian).
2. On the start screen, tap **Create a profile**. If you already have a profile, tap **Sign in**.
3. Accept the user agreement and fill in the **Your details** screen: profile type, name, date of birth, sport category, country. Tap **Next**.
4. Enter your email, tap **Get the code**, and type the 6 digits from the message. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder.
5. Wait for the data to load and tap **Continue**.

## App introduction

There are three sections at the bottom: **Home**, **Calendar**, and **Profile**. The home screen has **My weapons**, **My records**, and **My statistics**. The calendar shows events by date, and **Profile** holds **Disciplines**, **Documents**, and **Settings**.

## Your first event

### Weapons

1. Open **My weapons** and tap **Add a weapon**.
2. Enter the name, serial number, and weapon type.
3. Open the weapon and, under **Linked exercises**, link the disciplines you shoot. Tap **Save links**.

### Event and series

1. In **Calendar**, tap **Create an event**.
2. Enter the **Name**, pick the **Date**, **Discipline**, and **Weapon**.
3. Under **Sessions**, tap **Create a session** and choose a type, for example **Control**.
4. Open a target card and mark the shots of your series on the target.
5. Check the targets on the **Target review** screen, tap **Save the session**, then **Done**.

> [!note] Empty targets
> The app deletes targets without marks when you save.
