An event is no longer a single line in your diary: it is a set of sessions, every target is analyzed for grouping and distribution, and the result is checked against the rank standard. The version has 14 new features and 8 fixes. The update is free, and all your records are preserved.

## What's new

### The highlights

**An event as a set of sessions.** A single training day or competition is made up of several sessions; they are listed in the event card and counted together.

**Target check before saving.** A new final step: a summary per target with shots, average score, and accuracy. Targets with no marks left are removed automatically.

**Session statistics.** Total, average score, average grouping, hit distribution and a comparison of targets, right after input.

**Rank compliance.** The app checks the result of a test or competition against the standard: whether it has been met and how many points are left to the next rank.

**PDF report.** The diary turns into a report in one action, from the event list or the event card. The report stays in the app: open it later or send it to your coach.

### Sections

**"Disciplines".** A new reference: weapon, categories, stages, distance, number of scoring shots and target for each discipline. You can mark the ones you need as favorites.

**Your own files in "Documents".** Add your own PDFs to the rifle shooting materials, delete them, and share them from the app.

**Favorite disciplines only.** When linking exercises to a weapon, you can show a short list of favorites instead of the full reference.

### Profile

**Days left until the medical checkup.** The app counts the time and warns you in advance, not on the day the clearance has already expired.

**Age check at registration.** Date of birth is checked against the rules.

**License agreement at hand.** It opens from settings and the start screen; if a new edition is released, the app asks you to accept it.

### Appearance

**Updated design.** Nothing overlaps system elements; the interface looks the same on iPhone and Android.

## What's changed

**"All about shooting" became "Documents".** The same shooting materials plus the ability to keep your own files next to them.

**The bottom navigation bar has been updated.** Same three sections, smoother transitions.

**Target input and analysis have been reworked.** The order of actions is the same; marks, zooming, and grouping calculation are more accurate.

**Profile switching is now clearer.** Profiles on the device are in a separate list; delete the ones you don't need.

**Personal data is protected.** Records, weapon information and profile data are stored on the device in a protected form.

## What's fixed

**Deleting targets.** A target is deleted together with its shots, so no leftovers remain in the diary.

**Deleting weapons.** A weapon is deleted together with its linked exercises and corrections.

**Duplicate sight corrections.** Identical entries no longer appear in the list.

**Cut-off content on the screen.** Buttons and headings no longer go under the camera cutout and system bars. Skippable steps now have a hint.

**Signing in to the app.** The start screen has been rebuilt and sign-in fixed, so the app no longer gets stuck at launch.

**Safety of records during the update.** Records, weapons and profile from version 1.0.0 are carried over without loss.

**Consistent button appearance.** Primary actions look the same on all screens.

**Dates and labels.** "2 years", "5 years", and the names of months and days of the week are displayed correctly.

## Who this update is for

| Level | What SPM 2.0.0 gives |
|---------|--------------------|
