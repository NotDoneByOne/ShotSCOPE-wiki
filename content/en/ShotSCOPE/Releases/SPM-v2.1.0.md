The discipline reference grew from 7 to 23 events under the Russian Shooting Union rules, and every new discipline already has rank standards. The knowledge base opens right inside the app, and English is now available. The update is free, and all your records stay in place.

## New

### Disciplines

**16 new fixed-target events.** Rifle: ВП-20, ВП-30, ВП-40, МВ-30, МВ-60, МВ-3x40. Pistol: ПП-20, ПП-30, ПП-40, МП-20, МП-30СС, МП-60СС, МП-60М, МПП-30, МПП-40, КП-60. You'll find them in **Profile → Disciplines**.

**Rank standards under EVSK 2026.** New disciplines include MSMK, MS, KMS and I–III rank standards for men and women. The app checks your result against them just as it does for other disciplines.

**Stages in multi-stage events.** МВ-3x40 and МВ-3x20 are split into kneeling, prone and standing stages, and КП-60 into МП-30 and МП-30С. Stages are shown on the discipline card.

**Centerfire pistol.** A new weapon type for КП-60 — **Pistol (ЦБ)**. Pick it when you add a weapon.

### Knowledge base in the app

Articles from wiki.shotscope.ru open inside the app, with no trip to the browser. There's a section tree, search and an article outline. Articles you've read stay available offline.

### English language

Switch the interface to English in **Profile → Settings → Interface language**. By default the app follows the system language.

### Shot list on the target

On the target screen, shots are collected in a side panel: score, number and shot details are visible without leaving the target.

## Changed

**Look and feel.** The light theme is now layered: a grey page, white cards and soft shadows. The top bar is translucent, and the bottom edge of the screen fades into a gradient.

**Launch screen.** A new video splash in light and dark themes.

**Coach QR code.** The link from the QR code goes to shotscope.ru and opens the app right away if it's installed.

**Weapon serial number.** A weapon won't be saved without a serial number — the app will remind you to fill it in.

**Crash reports.** If the app crashes, it sends an anonymised technical report. Email and sign-in tokens are never included. This helps us find bugs faster.

## Fixed

**A ten was counted as a nine.** The ring reference now repairs itself, and the scores of saved shots, series and events have been recalculated.

**Target for МП-60.** You can add a target in МП-60 sessions again — the button used to do nothing.

**Empty session with no explanation.** If a session has no targets, the app tells you why.

**Bottom navigation bar.** No longer disappears while scrolling.

**Numbers on the target.** Ring numbers are centred, and the zoom buttons no longer cover the target.

## How to update

The update is available in RuStore: [ShotSCOPE](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.ndo.shotscope)

There's nothing to move by hand: records, weapons and your profile stay in place.
