ShotSCOPE SPM is a reworked app for everyone involved in shooting sports. Version 1.0.0 is completely free and brings a new design, weapon records, extended analytics, and an audio trainer.

## What's new

### Events

Save the results of your training sessions and performances. There are ready-made templates for different scenarios and linking to a weapon and an exercise.

### Calendar

The whole history of your sporting journey on a timeline: training sessions, starts, personal records. Keep the most important ones in a separate **Favorite events** section.

### Home screen

- Today's summary.
- Quick access to your weapons (**My weapons**).
- Latest entries.

### Analytics

- Automatic error tracking.
- Detailed statistics.
- Identifying weak spots.

The app doesn't just show numbers; it suggests what to look at next.

### Weapon records

Models, serial numbers, sighting, and sight settings for different exercises and shooting conditions.

### Audio trainer

Rapid-fire training without electronic equipment. Programs from 3 to 150 seconds.

### Athlete's knowledge base

- A library of exercises for all the main disciplines of Russian competitions.
- Rules and regulations in their latest editions.
- Athlete data: UIN, sport categories, medical check-ups, all in one profile.

### Security

Your personal data and weapon information are protected by modern encryption.

## Who this update is for

| Level         | What SPM gives                                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Professionals | In-depth analytics, progress control, reaching peak form           |
| Coaches       | Monitoring athletes, adjusting programs based on data              |
| Beginners     | A diary culture, discipline, structured growth from the first days |
