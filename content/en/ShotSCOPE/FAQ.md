Here are answers to the questions people ask most often about ShotSCOPE SPM. If you didn't find what you need, write to [notdonebyone@internet.ru](mailto:notdonebyone@internet.ru) or in the community Telegram channel.

## Features

### Can I plot result charts for EVSK exercises (date — result — comment)?

Yes, the feature is in development. Charts for EVSK exercises will arrive in one of the upcoming updates. Follow the news in the [[en/ShotSCOPE/Releases/_index|Releases]] section.

### Will there be a competition calendar?

Yes. A calendar of competitions and training sessions is one of the next features. You will be able to plan starts, mark important dates, and return to the results of past performances.

## Data

### How do I restore my data when I change phones?

For now, data is transferred manually when you change devices. We are working on cloud sync: it will restore your history of training sessions and competitions, give you access to your data from several devices, and keep your results even if your phone is damaged. The feature is expected in one of the 2026 updates.

## Countries and targets

### Why isn't Russia in the country list?

Russia is in the list. It sits next to Puerto Rico in alphabetical order: scroll the list further.

### Where can I find targets for the match pistol?

The match pistol target is in the **MPP-60** exercise. If the target type you need is missing, write to us: we will check and, if necessary, add it in the next update of the exercise database.

## Bugs and updates

### I found a bug in the app. Where do I report it?

Write to the technical support email: notdonebyone@internet.ru. We are a small team, and your messages help us find and fix issues faster. We try to reply to everyone.

### When will the next update be released?

We release high-quality, stable versions rather than chasing the number of updates. We announce exact dates in the [[en/ShotSCOPE/Releases/_index|Releases]] section and in our Telegram channel.

## General questions

### Is the app really free?

Yes. Since **SPM v1.0.0** the app is completely free: no hidden subscriptions or restrictions. Everything in the app is available to every athlete, from beginner to professional.

### Who is this app for?

ShotSCOPE SPM is made for everyone involved in shooting sports.

| Who           | What it gives                                                     |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Professionals | In-depth analytics, progress control, reaching peak form          |
| Coaches       | Monitoring athletes, adjusting programs based on data             |
| Beginners     | A diary culture, discipline, structured growth from the first days |
