This section covers what an athlete needs to know about anti-doping rules: obligations, the annual course, the Prohibited List, and the official resources of the Russian Anti-Doping Agency (RUSADA).

## Athlete obligations

Under the World Anti-Doping Code and the All-Russian Anti-Doping Rules, every athlete must:

- know and comply with the anti-doping rules;
- not use prohibited substances or methods;
- cooperate with doping control procedures;
- complete education every year and confirm knowledge of the rules.

## Annual course and certificate

Once a year, complete education on the RUSADA educational portal [course.rusada.ru](https://course.rusada.ru/) and get a certificate confirming you know the doping control rules. The course depends on age and status:

| Category | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| New athlete, no certificate | Complete a suitable online course from scratch on [course.rusada.ru](https://course.rusada.ru/) |
| Ages 7–13, 2025 certificate | In your personal account (through a parent account), confirm your knowledge in the "Values of Sport 2026" course |
| Age 14 and over, 2025 certificate | In your personal account, confirm your knowledge in the "Anti-Doping Course 2026" |
| Hearing-impaired athlete | Complete the "Course for Hearing-Impaired Athletes 2026" |
| Sports doctor or staff | Complete the "Course for Sports Medicine Specialists 2026" |

After the course and the test, you receive a personal RUSADA certificate.

## Prohibited List 2026

The updated Prohibited List has been in force since January 1, 2026. WADA (the World Anti-Doping Agency) reviews it every year, and RUSADA publishes it in Russian. The main changes:

- blood drawing is prohibited outside medical indications (exceptions: medical examinations, blood donation, doping control, PRP);
- hirudotherapy (leeches) and autohemotherapy are prohibited from January 1, 2026;
- α-naphthoflavone, BAM15, fladrafinil, and flmodafinil have been added;
- rules on glucocorticoids and elimination periods have been clarified.

Official Russian-language version: [[Антидопинг/rusada.pdf|Prohibited List 2026 (in Russian)]].

## Medication check

Before taking any medicine or dietary supplement, check it in the RUSADA service [list.rusada.ru](https://list.rusada.ru/): enter the name and see whether it contains prohibited substances.

## RUSADA resources and contacts

- [course.rusada.ru](https://course.rusada.ru/): online courses and certification.
- [ADAMS](https://adams.wada-ama.org/): whereabouts entry for testing pools.
- General inquiries: rusada@rusada.ru.
- Hotline (substance status): +7 (966) 183-47-94.

## Athlete's checklist

1. Complete education at [course.rusada.ru](https://course.rusada.ru/) every year and keep your certificate.
2. Check medications through [list.rusada.ru](https://list.rusada.ru/).
3. Keep up with updates to the [[Антидопинг/rusada.pdf|Prohibited List (in Russian)]].
4. If you are in a testing pool, fill in your whereabouts in ADAMS on time.
5. When in doubt, call the hotline or write to RUSADA.
